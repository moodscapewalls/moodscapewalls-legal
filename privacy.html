MoodscapeWalls TikTok Analytics — Privacy Policy

Data collected: basic TikTok profile information (username, follower count) and public video metadata (views, likes, comments, shares) obtained via the TikTok API (video.list, user.info.basic), limited to the account owner's own TikTok account.

Purpose: internal content performance tracking only.

Storage: data is stored privately and is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party, and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Retention: data is kept only as long as needed for internal reporting and can be deleted on request.
