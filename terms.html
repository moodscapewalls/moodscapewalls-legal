MoodscapeWalls TikTok Analytics — Terms of Service

This is an internal tool built and used solely by MoodscapeWalls (moodscapewalls.gumroad.com) to read performance data from its own TikTok account (@moodscapewalls) via the TikTok for Developers API.

It is not a public-facing product, is not distributed to other users, and does not act on behalf of any TikTok account other than the owner's own.

By using TikTok Login Kit, the account owner authorizes this tool to read basic profile info and video list data for internal reporting purposes only.
